Release notes

Help

What can I add?
  • Any book, by title: search Open Library or type the details. Log pages by hand.
  • PDFs and EPUBs to read here or have read aloud. Your pages are logged for you as you read.
  • Audiobooks: MP3 and M4A/M4B are the safest bets. Add several files at once and choose: one book or separate books.
  • Backups: a .zip or .json from this app, an older mbreading .csv, or an mbpdf .zip.
  • A file for a book you already track: open the book and choose Attach a file. Your notes and history stay.
How does automatic logging work?

When you turn pages forward in the reader, one page or one spread at a time, those pages are added to today's total. Jumping ahead with the slider, contents or search moves your place without counting the pages you skipped, and re-reading the same page on the same day only counts once.

EPUB page counts depend on your text size, so progress is measured in estimated print pages (about 275 words each). Audiobooks count 1 page for every 2 minutes you listen. Automatic sessions are marked auto on the book's page and can be deleted like any other.

How do I listen to a book?

Open it and tap the headphones, or press P. It reads on and turns the pages for you.

  • Set the voice, speed and sleep timer in the player.
  • In Text mode and EPUBs, the sentence being read is highlighted. Tap any sentence to read from there.
  • Pick an “On this device” voice to keep the text private. Online voices send it to their provider.
How do streaks work?
Any day you log at least one page counts. Your streak stays alive through today even if you haven't read yet; it resets if a full day passes with no pages logged. You can log for a past day using the date field.
Why won't a file open or read aloud?
  • Locked files: password-protected PDFs and DRM-protected EPUBs can't be opened.
  • Scanned PDFs open, but have no text to read aloud, search or quote.
  • Audio your browser can't play: convert it to MP3 or M4A.
Where is my data stored?

Only in this browser, on this device. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it, and it doesn't follow you to another browser or device, so use Save backup now in Settings every so often. In Chrome or Edge on a computer you choose the files once and each save updates them.

Privacy, terms and credits

Privacy policy

Short version: mbreading.online has no accounts, analytics, ads or cookies. Your books, files, notes and reading history stay in this browser, and the developer never receives them.

What's saved on your device

  • localStorage (“mbreading_state_v2”): your library list, reading sessions, notes, quotes, ratings, goals and app settings.
  • localStorage (“mbreading.reader”): reader display and player settings, and when you last made a backup.
  • localStorage (“mbreading_state_v2_before_3_0”): if you used mbreading.online before version 3.0, an untouched copy of your library from before the upgrade, kept as a safety net. Erase all data removes it.
  • Browser database (IndexedDB, named “mbreading”): the PDF, EPUB and audio files you add, their covers, your place in each, bookmarks and bookmark notes.
  • Browser database (IndexedDB, named “mbreading-saves”): if you choose where backups are saved, your browser's permission to update those backup files. It holds no copy of your data, and the page never sees the folder path.

This stays until you delete it: delete a book or its file from the book's page, use Erase all data in this browser in Settings, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Save backup now and Export All save copies to files on your device, and Import Backup brings them back.

What leaves your device

  • Book search: when you use “Find a book”, only the words you type are sent to Open Library (openlibrary.org, run by the non-profit Internet Archive). Cover images load from covers.openlibrary.org. Your library, notes and history are never sent.
  • Code downloads: your browser downloads the PDF engine (PDF.js) from cdnjs.cloudflare.com and the page-flip library from cdn.jsdelivr.net. When a PDF needs them, the PDF engine also downloads font and character files from cdnjs. These services see your IP address and browser type, not your files or notes, and this page tells your browser not to send them its address.
  • Read aloud: voices listed under “On this device” run locally. Voices listed under “Online” send the text being read to the company that provides the voice (for example Google or Microsoft), under that company's terms. An on-device voice is picked by default when there is one.
  • The website host may keep standard request logs (IP address, browser type, time of visit), as nearly every website does.
  • Links inside books open in a new tab only when you click them.

Selling, sharing and your choices

mbreading.online doesn't sell or share personal information and doesn't use it for advertising. The developer never receives your data, so they can't view, recover or delete it for you. Use the delete, backup and restore controls instead. Don't store passwords or other highly sensitive information in notes.

Children

mbreading.online is a general-audience tool, not directed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone.
