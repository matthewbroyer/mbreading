Help & privacy

Where is my data stored?
Only in this browser's local storage, on this device. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded. Clearing your browser data or switching browsers/devices means starting fresh, so keep a JSON backup.
What does the book search send?
When you use "Find a book" in the Add Book dialog, only the words you type are sent to Open Library (openlibrary.org, run by the non-profit Internet Archive) to look up title, author, pages and cover. Cover images load from covers.openlibrary.org. Your library, notes, quotes and reading history are never sent anywhere. Manual entry works fully offline.
How do streaks work?
Any day you log at least one page counts. Your streak stays alive through today even if you haven't read yet; it resets if a full day passes with no pages logged. You can log for a past day using the date field.
Privacy policy
mbreading.online is a free, client-side web app. It does not collect, transmit, or share your personal information, and uses no analytics, ads, or cookies. The only outside requests are optional Open Library book searches and cover images, described above. Don't store passwords or other highly sensitive information in notes.
