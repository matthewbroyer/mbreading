FAQ & Legal

Where is my data stored?
Everything is stored only in this browser’s local storage. Nothing is uploaded or sent to any server.
How do I back up or delete my data?
Use Export data for a CSV backup. Use Clear all data in the menu to erase everything stored by mbreading.online in this browser.
Privacy Policy

mbreading.online is a free, client-side web app. It does not collect, transmit, or share your personal information.

All data stays in your browser’s local storage. No accounts, analytics, or advertising.

Do not enter passwords, SSNs, credit-card numbers, or other highly sensitive information.
