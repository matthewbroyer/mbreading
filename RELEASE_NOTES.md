# Release notes · Reading (mbreading.online)

Newest first. One entry per release, in plain words a user would understand. The full technical log is in CHANGELOG.md, and the same notes show in the app under Settings & Help › Release notes.

## 1.2 · 2026-10-14
- On computers, the search box, backup status and logo spacing now look exactly like the other mb apps: same size, same "Search…" text and the same "/" shortcut.
- Tidier top bar that matches the other mb apps. On phones the app name no longer gets cut off, and search, Undo, Data and the light/dark switch are matching square buttons.
- The light/dark switch shows a sun and a moon so it's clear what it does.
- On computers, Data, Add book and Log reading keep their text labels at every window size, and Data shows a small dot when a backup is due.
- Your books, notes and history are untouched.

Internal build 3.7.0.

## 1.1 · 2026-10-14
- New logo, redrawn in the same style as the other mb apps (app icon, tab icon, share image and pages all updated).

Internal build 3.6.0 (the number used in backups and the CHANGELOG).

- About card at the bottom of Settings: version, release date, links and copyright in one place.
- Installing from matthewbroyer.com: the hub's Install button opens the app with a short install prompt.
- New "page not found" page that matches the app and links back to it.
- The Install card moved to the bottom of Settings & Help, just above the About card.

## 2026-10-13 · 3.5.0

- New look shared with the other mb apps: sections on the left on a computer, icons on a tablet, a bottom bar on a phone. The logo and name always take you back to Today.
- Share buttons on your streak, a finished book and Stats make a square picture on your device to save or send. Book titles stay off unless you turn them on.
- The installed app is now called Reading.
- Phones: search opens from the magnifier in the top bar.
- Today opens with a banner showing the date and today's pages.
- Your books, notes and history are untouched.
